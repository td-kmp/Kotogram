KTG-9 · chat list · prototype

The chat list's bar, with the connection state in its title

One bar, three platforms' manners. The iPhone takes Telegram for iOS's (ChatListNavigationBar): glass capsules, a search field that folds as the list scrolls. The Mac follows the macOS guidelines: no Edit, the title at the leading edge, search at the top of the list. Android takes Material's: a top app bar and a floating new message button. On all three the title gives way to the connection state while TDLib isn't ready. Scroll the list with the wheel or drag it; pull it down past the top.

iPhone: Telegram for iOS's bar, from the sources

  • Rows (ChatListNavigationBar.update): under the status bar 3, the buttons' row of 44 (placed 10 under the status bar), 9, the search's 60 and 2, then the folders and 3.
  • Capsules (NavigationBarComponent, NavigationBarContentView): the left one holds Edit, 17 medium with 12 each side; on the right new story and the proxy's shield (30 in a 44 cell, only with a proxy), 44 each, 16 from the screen's edges. A pressed button's content dims to 0.6.
  • New message alone, in blue (decided on 2026-11-13): its own capsule tinted with the accent, the HIG's .prominent ("Only specify one primary action, and put it on the trailing side"), 12 from the other capsule (navigationBarInterSectionSpacing). Telegram keeps all three in one plain capsule.
  • Search (NavigationBarSearchContentNode): a field of 44 with 16 at the sides, 8 above the zone's bottom, the placeholder 17 regular, centred. Flat, the folders' fill, round ends (decided on 2026-11-13); Telegram's is glass.
  • Scroll up: the bar loses up to 54; the search zone folds over those 54, the field shrinks and fades out in the first 40 of them, and the folders ride up with the bar's bottom. Past 54 the bar stays and the list goes under it, through the edge effect.
  • Pull down: the bar grows with the pull and the search and folders follow the list; in Telegram the stories open there (from 65 pulled, with a tap of haptics). We have no stories yet.
  • The title (ChatListTitleView): every state but ready puts its text in the title with the indicator; ready puts "Chats" back, without an animation.
  • The HIG agrees: actions belong in the toolbar, Edit as text ("actions like edit that aren't well-represented by symbols"), a search field inline above the list it searches. Apple has no floating button. Its own Mail and Notes put New and search in a bottom toolbar; Telegram keeps its tab bar there.

Mac: the macOS guidelines

  • No Edit. A Mac list has no edit mode: rows are selected with the pointer and ⌘ or ⇧, and act through a context menu and the menu bar. The HIG doesn't say "no Edit button"; Mail, Messages and Telegram for macOS just don't have one.
  • The title at the leading edge, after the window's controls ("Elements that let people return … and show or hide a sidebar appear at the far leading edge, followed by the view title"); the state goes there with the indicator, as on the iPhone.
  • Actions at the trailing edge: new message in blue on its own, as on the iPhone (the HIG's .prominent is macOS's too), new story and the shield in one group. Every one of them also has to be a menu bar command ("Make every toolbar item available as a command in the menu bar"): File ▸ New Message, ⌘N, in the app's menu.
  • Search at the top of the list ("Include search at the top of the sidebar when filtering content or navigation there"), a flat field with round ends that stays: nothing folds on the Mac.
  • Sizes are the laptop's own: a toolbar row of 52 under the window's controls, buttons of 32, a field of 28, folders of 32, the title 15 semibold. Read off nothing; to be measured against AppKit's toolbar.

Android: Material, and Telegram for Android

  • The bar is Material 3's centre-aligned top app bar of 64, the title 22; search and the shield as icon buttons at the end. The list goes under it through the same blur as on Apple (decided on 2026-11-13).
  • The floating button for a new message, Material 3's FAB: 56, 16 from the edges, corner 16. Telegram for Android (DialogsActivity, FragmentFloatingButton) has a pencil of 48, 20 from the side and 14 from the bottom, and a story button of the same size over it; here the story is Material's small FAB of 40. Both slide away as the list scrolls down and come back as it scrolls up.
  • The state (LaunchActivity.updateCurrentConnectionState): the bar's title is replaced by the state's text (setTitleOverlayText), without an indicator; "Connecting" carries a tap to the proxy settings. Here the indicator comes before the text as on Apple (decided on 2026-11-13), so the title is one composable for all: the common ProgressIndicator, each platform's own through its override (UIActivityIndicatorView on iOS, the arc elsewhere).
